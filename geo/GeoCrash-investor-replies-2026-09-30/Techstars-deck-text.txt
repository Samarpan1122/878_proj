Entry point
Start with one agency or engineering consultant review. Test a scoped paid pilot
before a recurring software, hosting and support offering; pricing and demand
remain unvalidated.
What earns renewal
A reproducible review process, useful engineering explanations and less analyst
effort. These outcomes still need measurement.
Differentiation to test
Source lineage, visible exclusions and exposure assumptions tied to each
screening result. Public data and standard statistical methods alone are not a
proprietary moat.
Potential durability
Approved data integrations, repeated use inside review workflows and externally
validated evidence. These are development objectives, not established assets.


USE OF CAPITAL / PROPOSED MILESTONES
A bounded first pilot, judged in advance.
GeoCrash | Prepared for Techstars | September 30, 2026
5 / 7
1. Agree the test
Secure an agency or consultant reviewer, approved data and one named network.
Agree baseline, review budget and acceptance criteria before evaluating results.
2. Audit the inputs
Check road linkage independently; resolve historical traffic exposure and
commercial reuse rights. Freeze the dataset and record exclusions.
3. Observe the work
Observe an engineer complete a real network review. Compare shortlist quality
and analyst time against current practice; document whether the buyer would pay
and what blocks adoption.
4. Make a decision
Measure analyst time, reproducibility and purchasing interest. Continue only if the
evidence supports a paid deployment, then test portability to a second network.
