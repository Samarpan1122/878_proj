What this service contains

Real historical fatal-crash observations from the Fatality Analysis Reporting System (FARS). The public service presents geographic aggregates, recorded crash factors, and a transparent state count model.

  • Fatal crashes only. Nonfatal incidents and near misses are outside this dataset.
  • No vehicle-mile denominator is available in this national explorer.
  • Historical records do not represent current traffic conditions.
